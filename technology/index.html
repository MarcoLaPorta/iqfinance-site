1.0 · Architecture

From a sentence
to a strategy you can trust

IQ Finance splits the job in three: a language model that understands what you want, a deterministic engine that tests it, and a vault that executes only what you sign. None of them can do the job of another.

01

Describe

Write your goal in plain words: what to hold, how much, how often to rebalance, how much loss you can accept. No forms, no codes.

02

Build

The model turns your words into an exact specification — instruments, weights, rules, limits. It is a proposal written as data, never an order.

03

Test

The engine replays the strategy day by day on five years of real prices, costs included, measures its risk and applies fixed limits.

04

Decide

You read every number — the worst one first. If you sign, your vault executes. If you don't, nothing happens.

LEVEL 1 · INTERPRET

The specialised model

Turns your sentence into an exact specification. It writes proposals as data — it has no access to money and cannot start an operation.

LEVEL 2 · DECIDE

The quantitative engine

Replays the specification on five years of real prices and measures it against fixed limits. What crosses a limit never runs.

LEVEL 3 · EXECUTE

On a public blockchain

Once you sign, the operation runs in your personal vault and is added to the chain as a new block — permanent, public, verifiable by anyone.

No programming needed

The precision of code, without writing it. You describe the goal; the system produces the program and shows you what it does in plain numbers.

Same rules, every time

The engine runs the code deterministically: same strategy, same data, same result. Every version is saved, so you can compare and go back.

Checked before it runs

Risk thresholds, an absolute 35% loss limit and a luck-or-skill test are applied to the code’s results. What fails never reaches your money.
