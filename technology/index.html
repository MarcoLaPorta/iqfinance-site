IThe product

What IQ Finance does

A quantitative engine that takes hedge-fund discipline beyond its usual boundaries, while always remaining non-custodial.

IQ Finance is a quantitative, non-custodial wealth management platform. Here is how it works: describe in a single sentence the goal you want to reach — for example a balanced exposure between tokenized tech equities and tokenized commodities, with a maximum acceptable loss — or choose to buy and sell assets directly, with no delegation.

If you describe a goal, a deterministic risk engine checks every proposal before it is executed: precise mathematical thresholds — if even one is not met, the operation simply does not go ahead. If you operate on your own instead, execution is direct and immediate, without that layer of verification, with the same non-custodial custody. In both cases, no operation starts without your signature — always staying inside a personal digital contract (the Smart Vault) that belongs exclusively to you, never in an account we manage.

Two ways to use it, then: have the engine build and check a strategy from your goal — with ongoing management within your parameters planned for a later phase — or operate on your own.

Our ambition, stated plainly: we want to become the reference artificial intelligence for finance — the infrastructure on which private investors and institutions learn to rely, not yet another trading app.

IIOur two pillars

Why AI and blockchain, together, change the rules

Not two trendy technologies placed side by side for marketing — two guarantees that complete each other.

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

The AI interprets. The code decides.

A language model like Claude today interprets natural language with a flexibility that no rigid decision module in traditional finance has ever had: it grasps nuance, handles ambiguity, asks for clarification when needed. It is a real leap, not marketing — anyone who has tried to program an "if-then" rule system to interpret human intent knows how fragile it is by comparison.

But the same flexibility that makes an AI useful for interpreting is also what makes it dangerous to let it decide on its own: a probabilistic model can hallucinate, misread an ambiguous sentence, behave unpredictably precisely in the most volatile market moments. That is why in our system the AI has a single, clear task: translate intent into a verifiable data structure. It never decides whether to execute.

The next step, stated openly. Before the launch on the main network, this layer moves from a third-party model to open-source models chosen, configured and specialised by IQ Finance: a small model specialised in the financial domain does the work, with a larger model as backup for the requests the small one does not handle well, retrained periodically on real usage data. A different model changes the style, never the role: it remains subject, without exception, to validation by the deterministic engine. in development

BLOCKCHAIN & TOKENIZATION

A public ledger, not an entry in a private database

A share in a traditional account exists legally as an entry in a private database — that of the custodian bank, the clearing house. You see a number in the app; the authoritative ledger that decides whether that number is true is one you do not control and cannot inspect, and if the intermediary fails it becomes the subject of a long procedure before you recover anything — when things go well.

A tokenized asset on a public blockchain reverses this relationship: the authoritative ledger is public, anyone can verify it at any time, and ownership passes through a cryptographic signature that only you control — not the good faith of an intermediary. It is not an implementation detail: it changes the answer to the question "who decides whether this is really mine", from "an institution you have to hope is solvent" to "a protocol anyone can verify".

In practical terms this means a risk engine that can balance different RWA classes within the same Smart Vault, because they all live on the same verifiable ledger — not on incompatible systems that nobody reconciles in real time. The mechanism is direct: every token is issued and redeemed 1:1 against a stablecoin through a primary-market contract, with every issuance and redemption recorded publicly. Today, on the test network, tokens are issued and redeemed at par with USDC and do not yet track the price of the underlying asset: price tracking and custody of the real underlying with a regulated partner are requirements before any launch with real capital.

IIIWhy we exist

Why we exist

Not an abstract idea of "innovation" — a direct answer to a gap that has existed for decades.

A hedge fund can afford a team that validates every strategy before risking a single euro. An ordinary saver cannot — and so far has had to choose between blindly trusting someone else, or giving up active management altogether. We want to close that gap, without ever asking anyone to give up control of their funds to do it.

I

Non-custodial by principle

Custody always remains with the user. Not a temporary competitive advantage, but a constraint written into the contract itself: when an intermediary gets into difficulty, client funds often end up frozen or lost — our vault is designed so that only the vault owner can withdraw funds; an independent security audit is planned before any real money is involved.

II

Deterministic, not probabilistic

The AI interprets natural language. A rigid mathematical engine — not a language model, not a black box — decides whether an execution is admissible. Always verifiable, never an act of faith.

III

Real assets, diversified by nature

Equities, index ETFs, gold, oil and the dollar, all tokenized and verifiable on the same ledger — the same rigor a fund would apply to a diversified book, made available to everyone.

VTechnical deep dive

How it is built, in detail

What follows is not simplified marketing material: it is the real description of the architecture, with the same technical names, the same numerical thresholds and the same limits we would use in due diligence. If a fintech startup deserves trust, it must hold up at this level of detail too.

Level 1Interface & parsing

Translates intent, does not decide execution

The conversational interface is built on Vercel AI SDK with Claude as the interpretation model. Its sole task is to translate intent expressed in natural language into a rigorous JSON data structure — target assets, percentage weights, stop-loss thresholds, execution frequency. It is never the model that decides whether an operation can proceed.

This distinction is not cosmetic: in the code, the tool that submits on-chain executions is technically accessible to the model, but remains structurally powerless until a database row has already moved to the AWAITING_MANAGER_SIGNATURE state — a state reachable only after a real cryptographic signature from the user, verified off-chain against the registered wallet.

The power of AI, today and tomorrow. A generic language model like Claude is already able to interpret financial intents expressed in a natural and ambiguous way — a real leap compared to the rigid decision modules (the old "if-then" trees) that traditional finance has used for decades. Before the launch on the main network, Level 1 moves to proprietary AI: open-source models chosen, configured and specialised by IQ Finance. A small model specialised in the financial domain does the work, with a larger model as backup, reached through a router, for the requests the small one does not handle well. At launch the models are hosted by a provider and paid per use; dedicated rented GPU servers take over when volumes make them cheaper, which also keeps conversation data on infrastructure controlled by IQ Finance. The small model is retrained periodically on real usage data. A different model changes the style, never the role: it remains subject, without exception, to Level 2 validation. in development

Level 2Risk engine

Three independent gates, all fail-closed

Every proposal goes through three checks in sequence, each capable on its own of blocking execution. Gate 1 verifies direct risk thresholds: it rejects if the historical Sharpe ratio falls below -0.5 or if maximum drawdown exceeds the configured threshold (default 35%). Gate 2 is an absolute backstop, independent of any exemption applied elsewhere: if the backtest ever touched a permanent liquidation marker, the proposal is rejected regardless of everything else. Gate 3 recomputes the Deflated Sharpe Ratio server-side (Bailey/López de Prado formula) from raw data — never trusting a value the model might copy from a previous answer — with an acceptance threshold of 0.99 and a minimum of 750 bars of history (about 3 years).

The quantitative stack also includes Combinatorial Purged Cross-Validation and Monte Carlo simulations (10,000 paths) for estimating confidence intervals — computed and returned, even though today they are not part of the binary acceptance criterion of the three gates. Final position sizing uses the Kelly criterion, capped for prudence at a quarter of the full value (¼-Kelly), a standard practice to reduce variance compared to full Kelly.

  • DSR ≥ 0.99
  • Drawdown ≤ 35%
  • CPCV
  • Monte Carlo 10k
  • ¼-Kelly
Level 3On-chain Smart Vault

One contract per user, one role that updates everywhere

Each user receives their own Smart Vault, deployed as a minimal clone (EIP-1167 pattern) of a single verified implementation — cheap to create, impossible to alter individually after deployment. Two distinct roles govern the contract: the owner (the user) is the only one who can deposit, withdraw and define the router whitelist; the manager is resolved dynamically by reading the current address from the Factory on every call — a single manager-rotation transaction applies instantly to all existing vaults, with no need to re-initialize them.

The only function the manager can call is executeTrade, protected by a percentage cap on the balance per single trade and by an on-chain router whitelist — a compromised manager could route funds only to contracts pre-approved by the owner, never elsewhere, and never beyond the configured threshold.

  • EIP-1167 clone
  • RBAC owner/manager
  • Router whitelist
  • Per-trade cap
Real assetsMint & redeem

Tokenized securities, transparent mint and redeem on-chain

RWA assets are minted and redeemed 1:1 against a stablecoin through a dedicated primary-market contract, with an explicit checks-effects-interactions pattern (the token is burned before the counterpart transfer, to prevent reentrancy attacks on the reserve). The current catalog counts 17 RWA tokens, all verifiable on-chain, deployed on a test network. Today, on the test network, tokens are issued and redeemed at par with USDC and do not yet track the price of the underlying asset: price tracking and custody of the real underlying with a regulated partner are requirements before any launch with real capital.

Usage tokenExtra usage, in the wallet

A utility token for extra usage future

Use of the AI and of computation is measured in usage tokens. The amount included in a plan renews every month and is an internal credit: it is not a crypto-asset and cannot be transferred. When it runs out, the user will be able to buy extra usage tokens: a utility token issued by IQ Finance, which only gives access to IQ Finance's own service.

Technically it is an ERC-20 token on Arbitrum: issued at purchase and burned at consumption through a spending authorization signed by the user (EIP-2612), without IQ Finance ever taking custody of it. It stays in the user's wallet and can be transferred between wallets.

To remain a utility token, it follows strict constraints: no peg and no promised value, no reserve and no right of redemption; IQ Finance never buys tokens back to support their price; it is sold only while the service exists, from the launch on the main network; no request or announcement of listing on trading platforms; and no internal marketplace matching buyers and sellers. The qualification as a utility token is to be confirmed with our regulatory advisor before launch.

  • ERC-20 on Arbitrum
  • Burn on use · EIP-2612
  • No peg · no reserve · no redemption
  • No listing · no internal market
Scenario explorerPlanned for launch

“What happens to my allocation if…” in development

A module that helps reason before deciding: questions in natural language, historical scenarios replayed on real market episodes in the catalog's data (crashes, rate rises, strong-dollar phases) with drawdown, recovery time and each asset's contribution, hypothetical shocks to the factors the catalog is exposed to (US equities, technology, gold, oil, dollar, volatility), and comparisons between allocations under the same scenario.

It is kept separate from execution by design: it reads market data only and has no access to any execution tool; its output can become a proposal only by going back through the normal flow — backtest, risk engine, signature; every result is labelled as exploratory AI analysis, not a forecast and not a trading signal; historical scenarios are computed by the quantitative engine, and the AI explains the numbers, it does not produce them.

HonestyStated limits

What isn't true yet, said before you ask

The system currently operates exclusively on Arbitrum Sepolia, a test network: no real funds have ever been moved. The contract code is not yet publicly verified on a block explorer, and has not received a security audit from an independent third party — both steps explicitly planned before any launch with real capital, not details postponed by chance. Today your signature is verified by our backend; verification directly in the contract, including the minimum price you approved, is planned for a later phase. We consider transparency about these limits as much a part of the product as the code itself.

VIRoadmap

Where we're going

Seven phases, from today's technical verification to a quantitative engine for funds and banks too.

  1. 00

    Foundations and compliance

    Legal opinion, qualification of the usage token, external security audit of the contracts, incorporation of the company.

  2. 01

    Proprietary AI

    A small vertical model and a large model with routing, paid per use and then on dedicated servers; retraining on the financial domain with real usage data.

  3. 02

    Mainnet

    Launch on the main network with the token-based plans (Pro and Pro Max), the usage token and the scenario explorer: the first real users and the first verifiable track record.

  4. 03

    Scaling and global holding

    Simplified onboarding, new markets, an international corporate structure.

  5. 04

    Direct tokenization

    Direct issuance of tokenized real-world assets: an extension of the mint/redeem infrastructure already built, becoming the tokenization channel ourselves for third-party issuers, once the user base and the track record of the previous phases are consolidated.

  6. 05

    Proprietary blockchain and native DEX

    A blockchain of our own and a native DEX, to internalize the transactions and the technologies behind the product.

    Why: today every execution goes through third-party infrastructure — the network, the assets' primary markets, the oracles. A blockchain of our own and a native DEX would bring settlement, the liquidity of tokenized assets and the usage token inside IQ Finance, reducing costs for users and dependence on third parties. A long-term phase, after direct tokenization, not funded by this round.

  7. 06

    B2B — funds and banks

    The same risk engine, built for individual investors, made available as infrastructure to hedge funds and banks with digital exposures. The last phase.

The parallel that guides us: the great institutional risk-management engines were born as internal infrastructure, before becoming a product for the market. It is the same path we envisage — not a promise, a sequence of steps verifiable one at a time.
