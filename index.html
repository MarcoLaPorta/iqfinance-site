IQ Finance lets you describe in your own words how you want to invest: how much to put in large technology companies, how much in gold, how often to rebalance. An AI turns that request into a precise strategy, with exact weights and rules.

Before anything happens, the strategy is replayed on the last five years of real market data. You see how it would have behaved in good years and bad ones: how much it would have earned, how far it would have fallen at its worst, and how long it took to recover.

A separate engine with fixed rules then checks the result. If the strategy would ever have lost more than 35% from its peak, it is blocked and never runs. If it passes, the decision is yours: you approve it with your own signature, or you simply do nothing.

Your money stays in a personal vault that only you can withdraw from. IQ Finance never holds your funds. Most people either hand their money to someone they can't see into, or gamble on their own; we built a third way, the discipline of a quant fund with every decision in your hands.

Today IQ Finance is a working prototype on a test network: everything described here works with test funds, and no real money is involved yet.

You start with a sentence and end with a complete strategy and five years of its history. In between, two very different pieces of technology do two very different jobs, and neither of them is allowed to spend your money.

Describe your goal. Write it the way you would say it to a person: how much you want to invest, in what, and how often to rebalance. No forms to fill in and no financial jargon to learn.

The platform builds and tests it. An AI model turns your words into a precise plan: which instruments, how much in each, when to rebalance. A separate engine with fixed rules then replays that plan on five years of real market data. The AI suggests; it never decides.

Read the full analysis. You see what the strategy would have earned, its worst fall, how long it took to recover and every operation it would have made. It is a simulation on past data, not a promise about the future.

Your money never leaves your hands.

We never hold your funds: they sit in a personal vault that only you can withdraw from. Nothing starts without your signature.

  • An account only you own
  • Your signature on every operation
  • A public record anyone can check
Learn more

When you start, a personal vault is created for you: a small program on a public blockchain that holds your money and answers to you alone. It follows one rule above all: only its owner can take money out.

IQ Finance can propose operations, such as buying or rebalancing, but each one needs your signature first. And because the vault lives on a public ledger, every operation leaves a record that anyone can check.

Today this runs on a test network with test funds. Your signature is checked by our servers; checking it inside your vault comes later. An independent security audit comes before any real money.
