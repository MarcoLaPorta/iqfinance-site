Why we built it

Most people either hand their money to someone they can't see into, or try their luck on their own. We wanted something in between: the discipline of a quant fund, with every decision still in your hands.

Your money

Your money never leaves your hands.

We never hold your funds. They sit in a personal vault that only you can withdraw from, and no operation starts without your signature.

  • An account only you own

    A personal digital vault on a public ledger, not a shared account we control.

  • Nothing starts without your signature

    Every operation needs your approval, from your own wallet.

  • A public record you can check

    Every operation leaves a trace that anyone can verify.

Today this runs on a test network with test funds. Your signature is checked by our servers; checking it inside your vault comes later. An independent security audit comes before any real money.

What you can invest in

The markets that matter, in one place.

At launch you will be able to build strategies with the most important assets on the market. Every test uses real historical prices, adjusted for dividends and splits.

01

Stocks

Shares of the largest companies on the main markets.

For example Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Amazon, JPMorgan Chase and more.

02

Index funds

Whole markets in a single instrument, plus funds that follow the US dollar and market volatility.

For example the S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, Dow Jones and Russell 2000.

03

Commodities

The leading raw materials, often used to protect a portfolio when stocks struggle.

For example gold, oil and the other leading commodities.

Everything is held in your own vault, as tokenized versions of the real assets.

The catalog grows as tokenized versions of each asset become available.

On the test network today there are 17 instruments, as test versions that don't yet follow the real price.

If you need more, extra usage tokens stay in your own wallet.

When your plan's monthly amount runs out, you will be able to buy extra usage tokens. They are an IQ Finance utility token, a kind of digital voucher that pays only for the AI and analysis on IQ Finance. Each time the AI works for you, your balance goes down.

  • Your plan's monthly amount is always used first.
  • They stay in your wallet and you can send them to another wallet.
  • They are for using the service, not an investment: no promised value, no refunds, no exchange listing.
  • Markets stay open for your own orders even with no tokens left.

Plans and usage tokens start with the launch with real money. Today the prototype runs on a test network and nothing is for sale.
