06Plans at launch

Two plans.
One difference.

When IQ Finance launches with real money, there will be two monthly plans. They include exactly the same features; the only difference is how much AI and analysis you can use each month.

Pro

For regular use

€20per month, VAT included

Everything IQ Finance does, with a monthly amount of AI and analysis for building and checking your strategies.

Pro Max

For heavy use

€85per month, VAT included

The same features, with much more AI and analysis included each month, at a lower cost per use.

Included in both plans

  • Describe a goal and get a strategy
  • Full analysis on five years of real market data
  • Scenario explorer: “what happens to my portfolio if…”
  • Automatic management within the limits you approve
  • Your strategies and analyses saved
  • Buy and sell yourself in Markets — always, even when your monthly use runs out

Need more? Extra usage tokens, kept in your own wallet.

If you use up your plan's monthly amount, you'll be able to buy extra usage tokens. They are an IQ Finance utility token: a digital voucher that lives in your own wallet and pays only for the AI and analysis on IQ Finance. Each time the AI works for you, your balance goes down.

  • Your plan's monthly amount is always used first.
  • They stay in your wallet, and you can send them to another wallet.
  • For using the service, not for investing: no promised value, no refunds, no exchange listing.
  • Markets stay open for your own buy and sell orders even with no tokens left.

Plans and usage tokens start with the launch with real money. Today the prototype runs on a test network and nothing is for sale.
