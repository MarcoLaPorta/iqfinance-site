How it works

Four steps, and the last one is always yours.

01

Describe your goal

Write it the way you'd say it. No forms, no jargon.

“Half in big US tech, half in gold, rebalanced every three months.”
02

The platform builds and tests the strategy

An AI model turns your words into a precise plan: which instruments, how much in each, when to rebalance. Then a separate engine, built on fixed mathematical rules, tests it on the past five years of market data.

The AI suggests. The rules decide.

03

See what would have happened

You see how the strategy would have performed: what it would have gained or lost, its worst drop, and whether it passes our safety checks.

These are simulated results on past data, not a promise about the future. Rebalancing is applied inside the simulation; automatic rebalancing and watching a strategy after it goes live are coming later.

04

You decide. Your money stays yours.

If you like it, approve it with your signature. If you don't, nothing happens. Funds sit in a personal account that belongs only to you, never in an account we manage.

Before you decide

The full picture, in plain numbers.

Before you approve anything, you see exactly how the strategy would have behaved.

  • Growth over time. How €100 would have changed over the last five years.
  • The worst drop. The biggest fall from a high point along the way.
  • How steady the ride was. Whether gains came smoothly or in big swings.
  • Luck or skill? A statistical test checks whether the results hold up or could be chance. It needs at least three years of data.
  • Safety checks: pass or fail. If the strategy would ever have fallen more than 35% from its peak, it is blocked. If any check fails, it cannot run.
Your money stays yours

We never hold your money.

  • A personal account only you own. Each user gets their own digital vault on the blockchain, not a shared account controlled by us.
  • Nothing starts without your signature.
  • Every operation leaves a public record anyone can check. See an example ↗
  • Where we are, honestly. Today we run on a test network with test funds. Your signature is checked by our servers; checking it directly inside your vault comes later. An independent security review of the code is planned before any real money is involved.
